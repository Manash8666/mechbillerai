## 🎨 **BRANDING TEST** (1 minute) ⭐

**This is the STAR feature!**

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Click "Branding" in sidebar** (🎨 Palette icon)

2. **You'll see:**
   - Garage Name: ABC Motors
   - Default gold color (#C9A84C)
   - Upload area

3. **Upload a Logo:**
   - Drag & drop ANY logo with color
   - Or click to browse
   - Image preview appears

4. **Extract Colors:**
   - Click "Extract Colors from Logo" button
   - Wait 1-2 seconds
   - 🎨 Magic happens!

5. **See Generated Palette:**
   - Primary color extracted
   - 11 color variations created
   - Preview shows new theme

6. **Preview Changes:**
   - Right side shows UI preview
   - Your logo displayed
   - Buttons in your colors
   - Cards with your theme

7. **Save It:**
   - Click "Save Branding"
   - Theme saves to database
   - Applied immediately!

8. **Refresh Page:**
   - Press F5 or refresh
   - 🎉 **ENTIRE APP** now in YOUR colors!
   - Sidebar, buttons, everything!
